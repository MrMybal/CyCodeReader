## Projects and tools {#projects}

Reader opens project files as text documents. It does not interpret a `.sln` as an IDE, invoke UnrealBuildTool or provide CyCode’s debugger. Use CyCode for those workflows; Reader remains useful for reading, editing and comparing files.
